Stop sending
slop.

Slop is anything that asks for someone’s attention without giving them anything worth it. It’s not just a waste of time, it’s disrespectful. The fix isn’t always a better message.

  • Sometimes, write less.
  • Sometimes, say “I don’t know.”
  • Sometimes, say nothing at all.

1.Add something

What counts is what you bring to the conversation. Text you didn’t select, weigh or apply isn’t a contribution, however long it is or however long it took.

2.Stand behind it

People engage with what you send as yours: they ask why, push back, build on it. If you can’t answer, there’s no one for them to talk to.

3.Make it yours

The way a message looks says something before anyone reads it. Taking the time to write or rework something shows you engaged with it. Obviously generated text says the opposite, even when every word of it is right.
